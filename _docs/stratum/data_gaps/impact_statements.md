# Data Gap Impact Assessment Language

## ASTM E1527-21 Requirements
Data gaps must be identified AND their impact on the ability to identify RECs 
must be assessed. Simply noting "records unavailable" is insufficient.

## Required Structure
Every data gap must include:
1. What records were sought
2. Why they were unavailable
3. The impact on REC identification (significant / not significant)
4. Justification for the impact assessment

## Standard Impact Statements by Gap Type

### Sanborn Maps Unavailable
**Significant Impact**: "Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps were unavailable for [period]. 
This data gap is considered significant as it limits the EP's ability to evaluate 
historical uses of the subject property and surrounding properties during [period], 
potentially obscuring RECs associated with historical industrial or commercial 
operations during the period of non-coverage."

**Not Significant**: "Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps were unavailable prior to [year]. 
Review of aerial photographs, city directories, and county records indicates the 
property was used for [use] during this period, limiting the significance of this 
data gap."

### Government Agency File Unavailable
**Significant**: "TCEQ file review was not completed for [facility]. This data gap 
is considered significant as regulatory files may contain documentation of releases, 
corrective action requirements, or other information relevant to REC identification."

### Owner Interview Not Conducted  
"The current owner/operator declined to be interviewed. This data gap is considered 
[significant/not significant] because [reasoning based on other available information]."

### EDR Report Radius Limitation
"The standard search radii established by ASTM E1527-21 were used. Sites beyond 
these radii were not evaluated. This data gap is not considered significant as 
sites beyond standard radii are unlikely to represent a significant environmental 
concern to the subject property given [distance/hydrogeologic context]."

## Common Data Gap Errors
- Listing data gaps without impact statements (ASTM non-compliance)
- Saying "not significant" without justification
- Failing to note Sanborn coverage gaps by decade
- Not assessing aerial photo coverage gaps pre-1940
